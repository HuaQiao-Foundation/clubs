# RC Georgetown — Past Presidents Roll

**Purpose:** Complete the historical record of RC Georgetown presidents by Rotary Year.
Please help fill in the **blank cells** (and correct anything that looks wrong).

**How to read it:**
- A *Rotary Year* runs **1 July → 30 June** (e.g. "2024-2025" = 1 Jul 2024 to 30 Jun 2025).
- "Past President (PP)" here means a **President of RC Georgetown specifically** — not the
  personal "PP" honorific someone may carry from another club.
- Some presidents served **more than once** (shown with both years).

**What we know** comes from the club history document (Presidents #1–25, 1983–2008) and
recent meeting minutes. The **2008–2020 stretch is the main gap** — those are the rows
most needing your input.

## Notes for the record keeper

- Once filled, current members' years go into the `georgetown-member-master` **Past President**
  column (Active / Honorary / Alumni tabs), then sync to the directory via
  `deno task google -- gt sync past-president --apply`.
- Personal "PP" honorifics from *other* clubs (e.g. Randal Eastman — Shanghai; William Fo —
  Penang) are tracked separately in the **Rotary Honorific** field, not here.
